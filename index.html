About CiteVerify & How to Use

Scientific Claim Verification Pipeline & Verdict Guide

Our Mission

CiteVerify helps students, researchers, and reviewers detect AI-fabricated references, numerical misquoting, and retracted citations. By combining client-side manuscript parsing with live REST queries to Crossref and OpenAlex, users can inspect source-linked evidence before grading or publishing—even when analyzing plain, uncited text.

How to Use CiteVerify (5-Step Workflow)

1

Upload

Drop a .PDF/.TXT or paste text with or without citations.

2

3

5

Review & Export

Confirm/override flags, add notes & print PDF report.

Understanding the 5 Verdict Badges

SupportedPaper verified and claim terminology/metrics align with the source abstract.
Partly SupportedPaper exists, but numbers/scope differ or full text is behind a paywall.
UnsupportedHallucinated/invalid DOI or unindexed work with no verifiable match.
ContradictorySource paper concludes the opposite direction of the cited claim.
Possibly OutdatedFlagged for formal retraction status or publication age (>10 years).
